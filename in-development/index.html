In Development.

Games I’m still building. Most of them are playable in your browser, against bots or with friends at an online table. Each page ends with a few questions, and your answers come straight to me.

Sentai

Each act’s monsters line up face up, so you know what’s coming this act. Add one or two cards to a Run, a Set or a Color. Miss and the cards come back, but everyone discards two.

  • 2–4 Players
  • 20–30 min
  • Cooperative · Shedding

Slipstream

Everyone picks the same few speed cards in secret. Play one fast and hope nobody else did, or play it safe. Slipstreams can carry you right off the edge.

  • 3–6 Players
  • 15–20 min
  • Racing · Simultaneous Selection

Day One

Gather earth, then carry the stack across the sea, dropping one disc on every space you enter. Drop earth on a rival’s shrine and the sky rewards your generosity.

  • 30–45 min
  • Mancala · Area Majority

Polar Bear Market

Pitch for turn order, then place agents on the floes, spend them to hire stronger ones, or use a specialist’s one-shot trick. Three seasons of area majority.

  • 45–75 min
  • Bag Building · Area Majority
