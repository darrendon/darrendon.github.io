Mr. Magpie

Mr. Magpie has a nest full of shiny things and a larder to fill before winter. The woodland critters can’t resist a sparkle, so every autumn they trade him their winter acorns for his treasures. He keeps every acorn, win or lose, and whoever is lowest at the first snow doesn’t make it through winter.

How it plays

The rules in brief

  1. Same stashEvery critter starts with the same 13 acorn cards, one each from 1 up to 22, hidden in hand. You can’t make change, so the card you reach for matters.
  2. Mr. Magpie’s treasureMr. Magpie shows off the top treasure. Starting with the start player, beat the top offering by adding acorn cards, or pass for good. Nobody has to open; if everyone passes, he keeps it.
  3. He keeps every acornWin or lose, your offering goes face down into his larder. The last critter in wins the treasure, even if nobody else brought anything.
  4. BaublesMr. Magpie never sends a guest home empty-handed. Drop out after offering and take one bauble from the row for every acorn card you brought. Whoever drops out first picks first. Earrings, berries, buttons and feathers each score their own way; lost acorns go back in your hand.
  5. FrostsFour treasures are frosts: everyone brings a secret offering at once. The biggest wins, and everyone else who brought one takes baubles, biggest offering first.
  6. The first snowThe fourth frost ends the game at once. The critter with the fewest acorns left doesn’t make it through winter. Most points among the rest wins.

What I’m looking at right now: Whether the wide spread of acorn cards makes every offering a real choice, whether sticking someone with a treasure they didn’t want happens at the table, and how often treasures get skipped when nobody wants to open.
