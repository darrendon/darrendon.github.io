Slipstream

A game of chicken. Race to the cliff edge, and the rider who stops closest without going over wins.

Players
3–6
Time
15–20 min
Genre
Racing · Simultaneous Selection
Play in your browserGive feedbackRequest a kit

How it plays

The rules in brief

  1. Pick in secretEveryone has the same six speed cards. Before each race, pick three of them in secret. Each seat starts a different distance from the edge, so everyone can see roughly how far each rider wants to go.
  2. Lead a cardIn turn, lead one of your picked cards and say whether you play it risky or safe. Everyone else who picked the same card must play it too, risky or safe.
  3. CaughtSafe moves the small number. Risky moves the big number, but only for the last rider to play that card risky. Anyone who went risky before them is caught and moves just 1.
  4. The roadLand exactly on a boost and move 1 more. Land on a pothole and drop back 2.
  5. SlipstreamFinish right behind another rider and you slipstream past them to the next open space. Every move can trigger it, and it can carry you over the edge.
  6. Closest winsWhen every card is played, the rider closest to the edge without going over wins the race. First to two race wins takes the game.

What I’m looking at right now: Whether reading what your rivals picked is the fun part, and whether slipstreams make races swing enough without feeling random.
