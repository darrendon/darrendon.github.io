Doodle Relay

A drawing relay where only the first person knows what the picture is. Everyone starts a doodle of their secret word, then every few seconds the boards pass and you keep drawing someone else’s picture without knowing what it’s meant to be.

Players
3–8
Time
15–25 min
Genre
Party · Drawing · Cooperative
For fans of

How it plays

The rules in brief

  1. Your secret wordEveryone gets a card with six words, and one die picks the row, so everyone draws the same row of their own card. Flip the card for categories: the die picks a category and you think of your own word in it.
  2. DrawEveryone starts at once and draws their word for 10 seconds. One marker, no eraser.
  3. PassEvery board passes left. Now you’re holding someone else’s drawing and you don’t know the word. Keep drawing whatever you think it is. Each board gets three drawers.
  4. Guess togetherGo through the boards one at a time. The whole table agrees on one answer, while the player who started that board stays quiet.
  5. The revealFlip the board to see the word. Each board you get is a point for the team. Play a few rounds and try to beat your best.

What I’m looking at right now: Whether 10 seconds a pass is the right squeeze, whether three drawers per board is the sweet spot or the full lap is funnier, and whether the middle drawers steer the guess too hard.
