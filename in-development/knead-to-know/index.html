Knead to Know

It’s the night shift at the bakery, and nobody passes on the order tickets. Each decorator starts a cake from their own secret order, then the shift changes and the next decorator keeps going without ever seeing the ticket.

Players
3–8
Time
15–25 min
Genre
Party · Drawing · Cooperative

How it plays

The rules in brief

  1. Take the ordersSomeone picks an order number from 1 to 6, then everyone takes an order ticket and decorates that order from their own ticket. Flip the tickets for themes: the customer names a theme and you decide what goes on the cake.
  2. DecorateEveryone starts at once and pipes their order onto a cake for 10 seconds. One piping bag, no scraping it off.
  3. Shift changeEvery cake passes left. Now you’re finishing someone else’s cake and you never saw the ticket. Keep decorating whatever you think it is. Each cake gets three decorators.
  4. The front counterGo through the cakes one at a time. The whole crew agrees on what the customer ordered, while the decorator who took that order stays quiet.
  5. Open upRead the ticket. Every order you get right is a happy customer. Play a few nights and try to beat your best.

What I’m looking at right now: Whether the orders are pitched right (everyday things that are too much to finish in one 10-second shift), whether three decorators per cake is the sweet spot or the whole crew is funnier, and whether the middle decorators steer the guess too hard.
