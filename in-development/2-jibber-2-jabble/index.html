2 Jibber 2 Jabble

The sequel to Jibber Jabble. Instead of spelling a clue one letter at a time, your team builds a question one word at a time.

Genre
Party · Word
For fans of
Jibber Jabble
Give feedbackRequest a kit

How it plays

The rules in brief

  1. Draw a cardOne player is the guesser. Everyone else sees the card: five words, one each for Who?, What?, When?, Where? and Why?
  2. One word eachGoing around the table, each player adds one word to the question. A Who question starts with “Who”, a Where question with “Where”, and so on.
  3. Hmm?Every question ends with “hmm?” Once someone says it, the guesser answers.
  4. Next wordGet it right and move on to the next word on the card.

Who: Abraham Lincoln → Who is on the penny, hmm?
