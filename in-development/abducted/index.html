Abducted

A social deduction game where everyone knows your team except you. Your card faces out, so the only way to learn your side is to decide who to believe.

Players
5–12
Time
15–25 min
Genre
Social Deduction

How it plays

The rules in brief

  1. Cards face outShuffle the 10 Human and 10 Alien cards and deal one face down to each player, who stands it facing out without looking. You can read every card in town except your own. The rest are the deck. Everyone takes a Transmit and a Jam.
  2. SplitThe Town Leader lays the two split cards on the table to cut the town into two parties, as evenly as possible, and not the same way as last round.
  3. VoteOn three, everyone except the Town Leader holds up 1 or 2 fingers. The party with more votes goes out searching. The Town Leader breaks ties.
  4. SignalEach searcher secretly plays Transmit if they think they’re an Alien, or Jam if they think they’re Human. Shuffle and reveal: the majority scores for its team. On a tie, flip the deck’s top card, and its team scores.
  5. Check out that lightThe Lookout points at one searcher. That player looks at their card and the deck’s top card, mixes them face down and stands one without looking. The other goes under the deck. Pass the Town Leader and Lookout cards clockwise.
  6. WinningThe first team to 5 wins. Everyone looks at their own card: if it shows the winning team, you win. Say anything you like, but never show anyone their own card.

What I’m looking at right now: Whether players can keep up with who is on which side as people get abducted, and how the game feels with 5 players compared with 12.
