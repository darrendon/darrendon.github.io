Polar Bear Market

Every agent you hire is your money, your muscle on the map and your one-shot trick, and each one only gets used one way.

Players
2–5
Time
45–75 min
Genre
Bag Building · Area Majority
Board
Seven regions around the Midfloe
Play in your browserGive feedbackRequest a kit

How it plays

The rules in brief

  1. PitchEveryone plays a card from 1–5 at once. High cards act first, low cards draw bonus tiles, and in the closing round of each season the order flips.
  2. Two different actionsPlace, Hire or Activate, each once a turn, then draw back up to 5.
  3. Place or outbidOpen offices in regions within reach, or beat a weaker tile anywhere by paying the difference. The covered tile goes back to its owner.
  4. Build your bagSpend tiles as money to hire stronger agents and one-shot specialists. The market grows every season.
  5. Score each seasonSpring pays 1st place in each region, Summer the top two, Autumn the top three. Most VP after Autumn wins.

What I’m looking at right now: Whether choosing between placing, spending and activating a tile stays a hard choice, and how crowded the board gets at 4–5 players.
