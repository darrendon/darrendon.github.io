Day One

The world’s first day. Young gods carry earth across an empty sea, sowing it space by space to raise the first islands.

Players
2–5
Genre
Sowing · Area Majority

How it plays

The rules in brief

  1. Gather earthTop up the stack under your god with discs of one land: Fire, Forest, Shore, Reef or Stone.
  2. Walk the seaCarry the whole stack one space at a time in any direction, dropping the bottom disc on each space you enter. Your god comes to rest last.
  3. OfferingsA disc dropped on a rival’s shrine grows their island and their mastery of that land, and the sky gives you a point of favour.
  4. SurgeCome to rest on a stack holding the land you gathered and it surges: collect its top disc, add one of yours and walk again.
  5. Raise a shrineCollect the top disc where you stop and raise a shrine. Fill all three spaces around a peak to name it.
  6. NightfallWhen the earth runs out, the gods furthest along each land’s mastery track score, plus stars, favour and peaks.

What I’m looking at right now: Whether offerings to rivals feel like a real choice, and how quickly the earth runs out at each player count.
