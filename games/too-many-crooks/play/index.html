How it plays

The idea. You can't use your own people on a job. You crew up from contacts your rivals send over, and they crew up from yours. Whoever runs a job picks who goes on it and what each of them walks away with.

Roll. Everyone rolls in the open: 8 dice on job 1, then 7, then 6.

Send. In turn, send exactly 4 dice to rivals' jobs — any split, never your own. What you didn't send is what you have to pay people with.

Crew. Every crew is one of each kind: an inside man (a 1 or 2), a getaway driver (3 or 4) and the muscle (5 or 6). Never two of a kind. You score the total of your crew.

Cuts. At the same time and in secret, hand out your kept dice to your crew: 3 of them on job 1, 2 on jobs 2 and 3. Stack several on one person if you like. Whoever gets a die scores it. Anyone on your crew you don't pay gets nothing.

Your cut. On jobs 1 and 2 one kept die is left over — score it, it's yours. On job 3 both go out, and your crew scores double.

Promises. Say anything, agree anything. None of it binds you. There are two more jobs after this one, and everyone chooses who they send to next time.
