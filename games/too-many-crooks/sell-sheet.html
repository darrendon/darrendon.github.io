The game

You can't use your own people on a job — they're known. So you crew up from contacts your rivals send over, and they crew up from yours. Whoever runs a job picks who goes on it and what each of them walks away with. Promises are made out loud, and none of them are binding.

Why it works

  • Your payroll is what you didn't send out. One pool of dice is your workers, your wages and your score. Send your best people and there's nothing left to pay anyone with.
  • One of each kind. Every crew needs an inside man, a driver and the muscle, so a 1 is the die a job can't run without — not dead weight.
  • Promises out loud, cuts in secret. Everyone pays at once and reveals together. The whole table watched you lie — and there's another job after this one.
  • Three jobs, then the split. Short enough to play twice in a sitting. The last job doubles.
