Abducted. A social deduction game where everyone knows which team you're on, except you.

Your card sits in a stand, facing out. Human or Alien. You can see everyone's card but your own.

Each round, the Town Leader splits the town into two parties, and everyone else votes on which one goes out searching.

The search party secretly plays Transmit if they think they're an Alien, or Jam if they think they're Human. The majority scores for that side.

Then the Lookout sends one searcher to check out the light. They mix their card with one from the deck and keep one without looking. They might have just switched sides.

So everyone's trying to convince you what you are, and some of them are lying.

First team to five wins, and you win if your card is on that team at the end.

Just cards and stands in a tuck box. Five to twelve players, fifteen to twenty-five minutes.
