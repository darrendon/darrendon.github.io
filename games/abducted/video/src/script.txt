In Abducted, everyone at the table knows which team you're on. Except you.

Your card faces out in a stand: Human or Alien. You can read every card but your own, so the only way to learn your team is to decide who to believe.

Each round, the Town Leader splits the town in two, and everyone votes on which party goes out searching.

The searchers secretly play Transmit if they think they're an Alien, or Jam if they think they're Human, and the majority scores. Guess wrong, and you just scored for the other side.

Then the Lookout sends one searcher to check out the light. They mix their card with the top of the deck and keep one, blind. They might come back... different.

So your friends are telling you what you are. So are your enemies. And after the next abduction, they might have swapped places.

First team to five wins, and you win if the card in your stand is on that team, no matter how you played.

Fifty cards and twelve stands in a tuck box. Five to twelve players, fifteen to twenty-five minutes.
