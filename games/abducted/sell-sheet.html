ABDUCTEDSocial Deduction · 5–12 players

A social deduction game where everyone knows your team except you

Why it stands out
  • Your card faces out, so the whole table can read it. The only way to learn your side is to decide who to believe.
  • One possession every turn quietly changes someone’s side, so trust never settles.
  • Votes are thumbs up or down, at once: simple turns, big table talk, 5–12 players in about 15 minutes.

For fans of Two Rooms and a Boom and Hanabi

Face out

Your card stands facing everyone else. You see every card but yours; a few stay hidden in the middle, so counting never makes you sure.

Possess and split

The leader secretly swaps one player’s card with the middle, then splits the table into a Saucer half and a Cow half.

Thumbs

The next player beams up one half, which votes at once: up for Human, down for Alien. The majority scores. First side to 5 wins.
