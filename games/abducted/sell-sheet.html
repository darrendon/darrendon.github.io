ABDUCTEDSocial Deduction · 5–12 players

A social deduction game where everyone knows your team except you

Why it stands out
  • Your card faces out, so the whole town can read it. The only way to learn your side is to decide who to believe.
  • Every round the Lookout sends someone to check out the light, and they might come back on the other side, so trust never settles.
  • Fingers for the vote, one secret card for the signal: simple rounds, big table talk, 5–12 players in 15–25 minutes.

For fans of Two Rooms and a Boom and Hanabi

Face out

Your card stands facing everyone else. You see every card but yours; the rest of the deck stays hidden, so counting never makes you sure.

Split and signal

The Town Leader splits the town, everyone votes which party searches, and the searchers secretly play Transmit (Alien) or Jam (Human). The majority scores.

Check out that light
+

The Lookout sends one searcher toward the light: they mix their card with the deck’s top card and keep one blind. First team to 5 wins.
