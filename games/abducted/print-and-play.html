ABDUCTED

Print and play · 5–12 players · about 15 minutes · by Darren Don

Setup

  1. With N players, shuffle N Human and N Alien cards together.
  2. Deal one face down to each player. Without looking, stand it so it faces everyone else: you can read every card but your own.
  3. The other N cards go face down in the middle.
  4. Everyone takes a split card. Put the score cubes (any two tokens) on 0. A random player takes the Mothership.

A turn

  1. Possess. The leader swaps another player’s card for a random card from the middle, without them seeing, and shuffles the old card into the middle.
  2. Split. The leader splits the whole table, themselves included, into a Saucer half and a Cow half, as evenly as possible.
  3. Beam up. The player to the leader’s left picks a half, then flips the top middle card face up. It stays out of the game.
  4. Thumbs. On three, the beamed-up half votes: thumbs up for Human, down for Alien. The majority scores 1 for that side; a tie scores nothing.
  5. Pass the Mothership left.

Winning

The first side to 5 points wins. Everyone looks at their own card: if it shows the winning side, you win.

Table talk

Say anything, true or not. Never show anyone their own card.

What’s in the kit

24 team cards (12 Human, 12 Alien), 12 split cards (Saucer one side, Cow the other), the Mothership and the score card.

Printing. Print pages 2–11 double-sided, flipping on the long edge, on card stock if you can. Every card sheet is followed by its backs. Cut on the crop marks. For stands, use any card stands, or fold a 4 × 3.5 in strip of card into a tent and lean the card against it.
