Abducted

A social deduction game where everyone knows your team except you. Your card faces out, so the whole town can read it, and the only way to know which side you’re on is to decide who to believe. Some of you came back… different.

Overview

About the game

Everyone’s team card sits in a stand facing out: Human or Alien. You can read every card in town except your own. Each round the Town Leader splits the town into two parties and everyone else votes, with one or two fingers, on which party goes out searching. The search party secretly plays Transmit if they think they’re an Alien or Jam if they think they’re Human, and the majority scores. Then the Lookout sends one searcher to check out the light, and they might come back on the other side.

Everyone knows but you

The whole town can read your card. You only find out which side you’re on from what people tell you and what you can count.

Sides keep changing

Every round someone is abducted: they mix their card with the deck’s top card and keep one without looking. The friend who told you the truth last round might be on the other side now.

Cards and stands

50 cards and 12 stock card stands in a tuck box. 5–12 players in 15–25 minutes.

You seeEvery other player’s team card, how everyone votes, and how many Transmits and Jams the search party played.
You don’t seeYour own card, the deck, or who played which signal. When you’re abducted you see two cards, but keep one blind.
You winIf the card in your stand is on the first team to score 5 when the game ends.

Components

Ten Humans, ten Aliens

A team card reads from across the table: one colour, one word, one face. Deal one to each player; the rest are the deck. Every player also holds a Transmit and a Jam, so nobody can tell which one you played.

How it plays

Setup

Shuffle the 10 Human and 10 Alien cards. Deal one face down to each player, who puts it in their stand facing out without looking. The rest are the deck. Everyone takes a Transmit and a Jam. A random player is the Town Leader; the player across the table is the Lookout.

Split

The Town Leader lays the two split cards on the table to cut the town into two parties, Party 1 and Party 2, as evenly as possible. Not the same way as last round.

Vote

On three, everyone except the Town Leader holds up 1 or 2 fingers. The party with more votes goes out searching; the Town Leader breaks ties.

Signal

Each searcher secretly plays Transmit if they think they’re an Alien, or Jam if they think they’re Human. Shuffle and reveal: the majority scores for its team. A tie flips the deck’s top card, and its team scores.

Check out that light

The Lookout points at one searcher (not themselves). That player looks at their card and the deck’s top card, mixes them face down and puts one in their stand without looking. The other goes under the deck. Pass the Town Leader and Lookout cards clockwise.

Winning

The first team to 5 wins. Everyone looks at their own card: if it shows the winning team, you win. Say anything you like, but never show anyone their own card.
