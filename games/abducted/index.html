Abducted

A social deduction game where everyone knows your team except you. Your card faces out, so the whole table can read it, and the only way to know which side you’re on is to decide who to believe.

Overview

About the game

Everyone’s team card sits in a stand facing out: Human or Alien. You can read every card at the table except your own. Each turn the leader possesses someone, secretly swapping their card for one from the middle, then splits the table in half. The next player picks which half gets beamed up, and that half votes at once: thumbs up for Human, thumbs down for Alien. The majority scores for its side, so everyone is trying to tell you what you are, and some of them are lying.

Everyone knows but you

The whole table can read your card. You only find out which side you’re on from what people tell you and what you can count.

Sides keep changing

One possession every turn, unseen by its victim. The friend who told you the truth last turn might be on the other side now.

Cards and stands

38 cards, 12 stock card stands and two cubes in a tuck box. 5–12 players in about 15 minutes.

You seeEvery other player’s team card, the cards flipped from the middle, and who votes which way.
You don’t seeYour own card, or the cards left face down in the middle. Counting gets you close, never certain.
You winIf your card is on the first side to reach 5 points when the game ends.

Components

Twelve Humans, twelve Aliens

Each side has four characters, three cards of each, so a card reads from across the table: one colour, one word, one face. With N players, shuffle N of each side together and deal one to everyone; the rest go face down in the middle.

Rules in brief

How it plays

The full rules, ready to read or print.

Setup

With N players, shuffle N Human and N Alien cards. Deal one face down to each player, who puts it in their stand facing out without looking. The other N go face down in the middle. Everyone takes a split card; a random player takes the Mothership.

Possess

The leader (whoever holds the Mothership) must possess another player: take a random card from the middle, swap it into their stand without them seeing, and shuffle their old card into the middle.

Split

The leader splits the whole table, themselves included, into a Saucer half and a Cow half as evenly as possible, telling each player which side of their split card to show.

Beam up

The player to the leader’s left picks which half is beamed up, then flips the top card of the middle face up for everyone to see. It stays face up, out of the game.

Thumbs

On three, everyone in the beamed-up half votes: thumbs up for Human, thumbs down for Alien. The majority scores a point for that side; a tie scores nothing. Pass the Mothership left.

Winning

The first side to 5 points wins. Everyone looks at their own card: if it shows the winning side, you win. Say anything you like during the game, but never show anyone their own card.
