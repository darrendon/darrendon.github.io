Boo Who?

Everyone can see it but you.

How to play
  • This house belongs to the Ghosts. Some guests are still Living, and the Living don't belong. You can see everyone's card except your own.
  • The deck has one Living card per player plus a few more Ghosts, so any number of Living can turn up, including none.
  • Everyone has Boos worth 3, 2 and 1 (just the 3 and 2 at 4 players). Each beat, everyone places one Boo on another player at the same time. Each Boo goes to a different player, never yourself.
  • Then everyone decides at once: run (you think you're Living) or stay.
  • Right call (Living runs: Escaped. Ghost stays: Haunting): +1 per Boo in front of you, whatever the numbers.
  • Wrong call (Living stays: Scared to death. Ghost runs: Spooked): you score nothing, and each Boo on you pays its owner its number.
  • A game is one long night of three rounds: Dusk is a normal round. At Midnight, Boos go face down: you can see whose Boo it is but not its number, until everything flips at the end. In the Witching Hour, every point counts double.
  • Highest total after the Witching Hour wins; tied players share the victory.
