Boo Who?Bluffing party game · 4–8 players · 15 min

A bluffing game where
everyone knows but you!

Your role card stands facing the table, so you can read every card except your own. Each round, everyone puts Boos on each other, then everyone runs or stays at the same moment. A Boo is a point for you if you call it right, and a payout for whoever put it there if you call it wrong.

For fans of Scape Goat and Skull
