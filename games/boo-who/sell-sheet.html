The game

Your role card faces out, so everyone can read it except you. Players put Boo tokens on each other, then everyone decides at once: run or stay. The Living should run; the Ghosts should stay. Your only clues are the Boos in front of you.

Why it works

  • Paranoia on the table. No secret chats or votes. A pile of Boos might be a warning, or bait.
  • Everyone acts at once. Boos go down on a count of three; everyone runs or stays together.
  • Scoring in one line. Right call: 1 point per Boo. Wrong call: your booers score their numbers.
  • Fits in a deck box. Every piece is card-sized and flat, even the cardboard stands.
