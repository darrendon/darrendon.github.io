321 BOO! BOO! BOO!
A 3, a 2 and a 1 for every player

A party game for 4–8 players · for licensing

A hidden-role game where everyone knows but you!

Your role card stands facing the table, so you can read every card except your own. Each round, everyone puts Boos on each other, then everyone runs or stays at the same moment. A Boo is a point for you if you call it right, and a payout for whoever put it there if you call it wrong.

Everyone puts a 3, a 2 and a 1 on three other players, all at once. Pile Boos on the Living, or bluff a Ghost into running.

Run or stay?

On “3, 2, 1, boo who?” everyone points at once: at yourself to run, at the table to stay. Living should run. Ghosts should stay.

Living, and you stayed? The 3, 2 and 1 on you pay 6 points to the players who put them there. Had you run, they'd be 3 points for you.
