Boo Who?

A bluffing party game for 4–8 players. Your role card faces the table, so everyone knows whether you’re Living or a Ghost except you. Judge from the Boos other players put in front of you, then run or stay.

Overview

The pitch

You can read every card at the table except your own. Each round, everyone places a 3, a 2 and a 1 on three other players at the same time. Then everyone points at once: at yourself to run, at the table to stay. Call it right and each Boo on you scores you 1 point. Call it wrong and each Boo on you scores its owner the number on it.

Where to put your Boos

A Boo scores 1 point for the player it’s on if they call it right, and its number for you if they call it wrong. Your 3 is worth most on someone who’s about to guess wrong.

Reading your odds

The deck has one Living card per player and at least as many Ghosts, and only one card per player is dealt. Count the Living cards you can see to estimate your own, then look at the Boos in front of you.

No fixed split

A five-player table might have three Living one round and none the next, so counting cards alone never settles it.

Everyone plays at once

Boos go down on a count of three, and everyone runs or stays at the same time. There are no turns, and a round takes about four minutes.

Components

Seventeen cards and a stand each

Nine Ghosts and eight Living. Four players use 4 Living and 4 Ghosts, five use 5 and 6, and each extra player adds one of each, up to 8 and 9 at eight players. Your stand holds your card facing out, so you only ever see its back.

In the box

  • 17Role cards, poker-sized: 9 Ghost and 8 Living, with one shared back
  • 8Player boards, each a card-sized punchboard: a two-piece stand that slots together, and a 3, 2 and 1 Boo in that player's color
  • 8Scoring cards, one per player: how a right and a wrong call score, and the three rounds of the night
  • 1Scorepad and a rules sheet, folded

Mockups show a proposed production version. Everything stacks to about 18 mm, so the game fits a standard tuck box. A deluxe edition could swap the cardboard stands for gilt frames with a slot along the top.

Rules in brief

How it plays

Witching HourEvery point counts double.

Deal

Build the deck from one Living card per player plus extra Ghosts: 4 Ghosts at four players, 6 at five, then 7, 8 and 9. Deal everyone one card face down. Without looking, set it in your stand facing the table.

Boo!

Pick one of your Boos in secret. On “3, 2, 1, boo!” everyone sets theirs in front of another player. Do it three times, a different player each time and never yourself. At four players you only have the 3 and the 1.

Run or stay?

On “3, 2, 1, boo who?” everyone answers at once: point at yourself to run, or at the table to stay. Then everyone flips their own card.

Right call

A Living guest who ran has escaped; a Ghost who stayed is haunting. Either way, score 1 point for each Boo in front of you.

Wrong call

A Living guest who stayed is scared to death; a Ghost who ran is spooked. You score nothing, and each player who booed you scores the number on their Boo.

The night

Play Dusk, then Midnight with every Boo placed face down and flipped before scoring, then the Witching Hour at double points. Highest total wins; tied players share the win.
