Boo Who?

A paranoia party game for 4–8 players. Everyone can see your card but you. Are you a Ghost, or a Living guest who should run?

Overview

The pitch

Your role card stands facing out, so everyone can read it except you. Each round, players put Boo tokens on each other, then everyone decides at once: run or stay. The Living should run and the Ghosts should stay. The only clues you have are the Boos piling up in front of you.

Paranoia, on the table

No secret chats, no voting anyone out. A pile of Boos might mean “we think you're Living”… or “we hope you panic and run.”

Everyone acts at once

Boos go down on a count of three, and everyone runs or stays at the same moment. Nobody waits for a turn.

Scoring in one line

Right call: 1 point per Boo on you. Wrong call: every player who booed you scores the number on their Boo.

Fits in a deck box

Every piece is card-sized and flat. The stands are two cardboard pieces that slot together, so the whole game fits in a tuck box.

DuskA normal round. Learn who likes to boo whom.
MidnightBoos go face down. You can see whose Boo it is, but not its number.
Witching HourThe last round. Every point counts double.

Components

Rules in brief

How it plays

The full rules are in the print-and-play PDF.

Deal

Deal each player one role card face down. Without looking, set it on your stand facing out. The deck has one Living card per player plus a few more Ghosts, so any mix can turn up.

Boo!

Everyone has Boos worth 3, 2 and 1 (just the 3 and 2 at 4 players). On a count of three, everyone places one on another player. Do it once per Boo, a different player each time, never yourself.

Run or stay?

On “3, 2, 1, boo who?”, everyone answers at once: point at yourself to run, or at the table to stay. Then flip your card.

Right call

A Living guest who ran escaped; a Ghost who stayed is haunting. Score 1 point for each Boo in front of you, whatever the numbers.

Wrong call

A Living guest who stayed was scared to death; a Ghost who ran got spooked. You score nothing, and each player who booed you scores the number on their Boo.

One long night

Play Dusk, then Midnight with Boos face down, then the Witching Hour where every point is doubled. Highest total wins; ties share the win.
