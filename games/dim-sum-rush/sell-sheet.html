Dim Sum Rush

A partnership climbing game for 2–6 players

Darren DonKansas City, MO
darren@whatsgoodboardgames.com

The game

Each server holds a cart of tiles in a fixed order and puts one tile face up on the trolley. Your team can use its trolley tiles in any combination; the other team can't touch them. Every tile you put out is an offer only your partner can take. Empty every cart on your side before the other team does.

Why it works

  • Teamwork without bidding. Partners coordinate through shared tiles, not calls or bets.
  • Passing is a real move. Fill a trolley spot or swap a tile to reshape your cart.
  • Comebacks built in. Chef's Specials let the team that's behind seize the lead.
  • Plays at every count. Teams at 4 and 6, plus a free-for-all mode for 2–6.
