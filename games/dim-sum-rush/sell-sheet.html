The game

Your hand, your cart, stays in a fixed order you can’t rearrange. Each player also puts one tile face up on their team’s trolley, and only your team can play from it. That’s how partners help each other. When every cart on one team is empty, the round ends and the other team takes penalty points.

Highlights

  • No bidding. Partners help each other through shared trolley tiles.
  • Passing changes your cart. Fill a trolley spot or swap a tile.
  • Chef’s Specials. Four of a number or a run of four beats any regular play.
  • 2 to 6 players. Teams at 4 and 6, plus a free-for-all mode for 2–6.
