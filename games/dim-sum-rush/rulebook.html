A partnership climbing game · 2 to 6 players · 30–45 minutes

Dim Sum Rush

The Sunday lunch rush has hit the teahouse. Baskets come off your cart in the order they were stacked, and nobody leaves the floor until every cart in the section is empty. Clear yours before the other section clears theirs.

Object of the game: be the first section to empty every cart. After three rounds, the section with the fewest penalty points wins.

In the box

84Tiles or cards: twelve dishes numbered 1 to 12, seven of each, in four colored bands. Dim Sum Rush is made with tiles or with cards and plays the same either way. 1Cloth bag, for the tile version. With cards, you shuffle and deal instead. 1Rulebook, including the Every Server for Themselves variant.

These rules are written for the card version. With tiles, you draw from the bag instead of dealing and stand your tiles in a row facing you; everything else is the same.

The backs

A card’s back shows how the dish is served, which tells everyone roughly how strong it is. Backs face out, so everyone sees the shape of every cart.

Bamboo steamer 1–3 Porcelain plate 4–6 Clay bowl 7–9 Dessert tray 10–12

Setup

The main rules are the team game for 4 or 6 players. For 2–6 players with no teams, see page 4.

  1. Split into two sections, Jade and Lantern: 2 servers each with 4 players, 3 each with 6. Sit so the sections alternate around the table.
  2. Build the deck: with 4 players, leave out all 10s, 11s and 12s; with 6 players, use all 84 cards. Shuffle it face down.
  3. Deal cards one at a time. Put each new card on the right end of your hand, without looking for a better spot, until everyone has 14 cards (4 players) or 13 (6 players).
  1. The cards in your hand, in the order you were dealt them, are your cart. You may never rearrange it. Hold it in a fan with the backs facing out. With tiles, stand your tiles in a row facing you.
  2. Everyone chooses one card from their cart and places it face down in front of them. Reveal them together. This is your spot on your section’s trolley.
  3. The last person to eat dim sum leads the first trick.

Your cart and the trolley

Your cart is your hand. Its order never changes, except when you swap a card in from the trolley and slide it into a spot of your choice (see Passing).

Your section’s trolley is the face-up cards in front of you and your teammates. Each server has one trolley spot, and it always holds one card.

  • Trolley cards are never played. They are there to swap into your cart when you pass.
  • You may swap only with your own section’s spots, never the other section’s.
  • You only ever give away a card from either end of your cart. Watch what your teammates put out: it may be just what you need.

Table talk: don’t show or describe your cards. You may say how many you hold.

Playing a round

A game is 3 rounds. Each round is a series of tricks, and it ends the moment every server in one section has an empty cart.

Leading

The leader plays any combination from their cart to the middle of the table. The leader can’t pass.

Following

Going clockwise, each other player either plays a stronger combination or passes. A combination is stronger if it is:

  • the same kind and the same number of cards, with an equal or higher number (runs compare by their highest card), or
  • a Chef’s Special played on anything that isn’t a Chef’s Special.

On the run 3 4 5 you may play 3 4 5 or 6 7 8, but not 4 5: that’s a different size. You may play over your own teammate.

Chef’s Specials

Four of the same number, or a run of four, side by side in your cart. Play one on your normal turn, or lead with one. It beats any combination that isn’t a Chef’s Special. To beat a Chef’s Special, play a stronger one:

  1. A set of four beats a run of four.
  2. Same kind: an equal or higher number wins.
7 7 7 7 beats any pair or run of three. It is beaten by 7 7 7 7 or 9 9 9 9, but not by a run of four such as 2 3 4 5.

Passing

If you don’t play, you pass. Once you pass, you’re out of the trick until the next one begins. When you pass you may swap:

  1. Place the card from either end of your cart face up on one of your section’s trolley spots.
  2. Slide the card that was on that spot into your cart wherever you like.

Or just pass. Trolley cards are never played; swapping is the only way to change your cart.

Ending a trick

The trick ends when everyone else still in the round has passed. The player who made the last play wins it. Set the played cards aside face up.

The winner chooses who in their section leads the next trick: themselves, or any teammate who still has cards. Choose without discussing it. If the winner just played their last card, they still choose a teammate.

Getting out

When your cart is empty you’re out for the rest of the round. A swap never changes how many cards you hold, so the only way out is to play your last card. Your trolley spot stays on the table, and your teammates may still swap with it.

Round end and scoring

The round ends immediately when every server in one section has an empty cart, even in the middle of a trick.

The manager judges a section by its slowest server. The section that emptied its carts scores 0. The other section takes penalty points equal to the number of cards in its fullest cart. Trolley cards don’t count.

Bao plays his last card, and his teammate Jun is already out, so Lantern has emptied every cart. On Jade, Mei holds 3 cards and Lin holds 5. Jade takes 5 penalty points.

Next round

Record the penalty, shuffle all the cards for the deck back together, deal new carts and choose new trolley cards (Setup steps 2–5). The first lead passes to the left of the previous round’s first leader.

Game end

After the third round, the section with the fewest penalty points wins. If the sections are tied, they share the win.

A sample trick

Four players sit in order Mei (Jade), Bao (Lantern), Lin (Jade), Jun (Lantern). The trolley shows Mei 2, Bao 7, Lin 6, Jun 3.

  1. Mei leads 4 5 6, side by side and in order in her cart: a run of three.
  2. Bao plays 5 6 7 from his cart. Its highest card, 7, beats Mei’s 6.
  3. Lin passes. She has no run that reaches 7. She swaps: puts the 9 from the end of her cart on Mei’s spot and slides Mei’s 2 in between a 1 and a 3.
  4. Jun passes. Nothing on the Lantern trolley helps him, so he just passes.
  5. Mei plays 8 8 8 8, four cards side by side in her cart. It’s a Chef’s Special and beats Bao’s run.
  6. Bao passes. He has no stronger Chef’s Special. He swaps: puts the 1 from the end of his cart on his own spot and slides his 7 in next to another 7, making a pair.
Mei’s Chef’s Special

Lin and Jun already passed, so Mei wins the trick. Mei holds 6 cards and Lin holds 12, so Mei chooses Lin to lead next.

Clarifications

  • Your own spot: you may swap with your own trolley spot, just like a teammate’s.
  • Which card you give: only the card at the left or right end of your cart. The card you take may go anywhere, including either end.
  • Equal numbers: an equal number beats an ordinary combination, and an equal Chef’s Special beats a Chef’s Special of the same kind and size.

Every Server for Themselves

2 to 6 players, no teams.

Everyone plays alone. All the team rules apply, with these changes.

Neighbors’ trolley spots

You may swap with only three trolley spots: your own and those of the players directly on your left and right. With 2 or 3 players, that’s everyone’s.

Leading

The winner of a trick leads the next one.

Round end

The round ends immediately when any player’s cart is empty. Every other player takes penalty points equal to the number of cards in their own cart.

In a 3-player game, Ada plays her last card. Bea holds 4 cards and Cal holds 7. Ada takes 0, Bea 4 and Cal 7.

Game end

After 3 rounds, the player with the fewest penalty points wins. Tied players share the win.

Quick reference

Your turn: play a stronger combination, or pass. When you pass you may swap the card at either end of your cart for a trolley card and slide it in anywhere. Once you pass, you’re out of the trick.

Stronger: same kind and size with an equal or higher number, or any Chef’s Special.

Chef’s Special: four of a number or a run of four. A set beats a run; then an equal or higher number. Trolley cards are never played.

Never more than four cards in a combination. Never rearrange your cart.
