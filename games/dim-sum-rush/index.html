Dim Sum Rush

A partnership climbing game for 2–6 players, with a free-for-all mode, made for tiles or cards. Share them with your team, and empty every cart on your side first.

Overview

The pitch

Each team shares face-up tiles the other team can’t touch, so every tile you put out is a play only your partner can make. Your hand is your cart, in a fixed order you can never rearrange. The tile you put face up on your team’s trolley is how you help your partner out of a bad cart, and you’re always reading what the other team is setting up.

Teamwork without bidding

Partners coordinate through the tiles they share, not calls or bets. Scoring is one number: the losing team takes points for its fullest cart.

Passing is a real move

When you pass, you can fill an empty trolley spot or swap a tile. Passing is how you reshape your cart and set up your partner.

A way back into every trick

Chef's Specials, four of a number or a run of four from your own cart, beat any regular play so a team that's behind can take the lead back.

Plays at every count

Teams of 2 or 3 at 4 and 6 players, plus Every Server for Themselves for 2–6, where you share only with your neighbors.

Components

Twelve dishes, four bands

84 pieces, seven of each dish, as domino-thin tiles or as cards. Either way, the color of a piece's back tells you its range, so you always know roughly what an opponent is holding.

Rules in brief

How it plays

The full rules, ready to read or print. They’re written for the card version; with tiles, you draw from the bag instead of dealing, and everything else is the same.

Download the rules (PDF)View

Your cart and the trolley

Your cart is your hand, in a fixed order you can never rearrange. The trolley is the face-up tile in front of each player. You may use your own team's trolley tiles in any combination.

Combinations

Play a single, a set of 2–3, or a run of 2–4. Tiles from your cart must sit side by side, and a run's cart tiles must already be in order. No combination is ever more than four tiles.

Following

Beat the table with the same kind and size at an equal or higher number, or with a Chef's Special: four of a number or a run of four, all from your own cart. A set beats a run.

Passing

Passing takes you out of the trick. When you pass you may fill any empty trolley spot on your side from your cart, or swap a cart tile with a trolley tile.

Leading

The trick winner picks who on their team leads next, themselves included.

Scoring

The round ends the moment every cart on one team is empty. The other team scores penalty points equal to its fullest cart. Play three rounds; fewest points wins.
