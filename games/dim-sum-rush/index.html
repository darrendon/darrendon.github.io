Dim Sum Rush

A partnership climbing game for 2–6 players that works with tiles or cards. Teams share face-up tiles and race to empty every cart on their side. There’s also a free-for-all mode.

Overview

The pitch

Your hand is your cart, held in a fixed order you can’t rearrange. Each player also puts one tile face up on their team’s trolley, and only your team can play from it. The trolley is how you get your partner out of a bad cart. The other team can see it too, so they know what you’re setting up.

Partners help through shared tiles

There’s no bidding or calling. Partners help each other with the trolley tiles they share. At the end of a round, the losing team takes penalty points equal to its fullest cart.

Passing changes your cart

When you pass, you can fill an empty trolley spot from your cart or swap a cart tile with a trolley tile. That’s how you fix a bad cart and set up your partner.

Chef’s Specials

Four of a number or a run of four, all from your own cart, beats any regular play. It gives a team that’s behind a way back in.

2 to 6 players

Teams of two at 4 players and teams of three at 6. Every Server for Themselves plays 2–6, and you share trolley tiles only with the players next to you.

Components

Twelve dishes in four bands

84 pieces, seven of each dish, as thin tiles or as cards. The back of each piece shows how the dish is served, and each serving vessel covers a range of numbers. You can see roughly what everyone else is holding, but not exactly.

Tap a tile to turn it over.

In the box

  • 84Tiles or cards: 12 dishes × 7 copies, numbered 1–12 in four colored bands. Tiles are about 30 × 40 × 8 mm, with a bone face on a colored back. Cards are poker size, with backs in the four band colors.
  • 1Cloth bag for the tile version. With cards, you shuffle and deal instead.
  • 1Rulebook, including the Every Server for Themselves variant.

The mockups show tiles; the print-and-play kit uses cards. The rules are the same for both. Format, components, box size and finishes are all open to discussion.

Rules in brief

How it plays

The full rules are written for the card version. With tiles, you draw from the bag instead of dealing; everything else is the same.

Download the rules (PDF)View

Your cart and the trolley

Your cart is your hand, in a fixed order you can never rearrange. The trolley is the face-up tile in front of each player. You may use your own team's trolley tiles in any combination.

Combinations

Play a single, a set of 2–3, or a run of 2–4. Tiles from your cart must sit side by side, and a run's cart tiles must already be in order. No combination is ever more than four tiles.

Following

Beat the table with the same kind and size at an equal or higher number, or with a Chef's Special: four of a number or a run of four, all from your own cart. A set beats a run.

Passing

Passing takes you out of the trick. When you pass you may fill any empty trolley spot on your side from your cart, or swap a cart tile with a trolley tile.

Leading

The trick winner picks who on their team leads next, themselves included.

Scoring

The round ends as soon as every cart on one team is empty. The other team scores penalty points equal to its fullest cart. Play three rounds; fewest points wins.
