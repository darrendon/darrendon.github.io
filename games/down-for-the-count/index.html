Overview

About the game

In most climbing games you race to empty your hand. Here an empty hand knocks you out, so you have to choose when to play strong or weak melds to knock out your opponents and be the last one standing. On your turn, beat the move on the table with a bigger one or spend one of your three stamina cards: a reversal takes a card off the move, and a kick out puts a stamina card into your hand. Play your last card and you tap out. Need stamina with none left and you’re pinned.

Your last card is a liability

Your last card knocks you out, so you’re always weighing a big play now against having nothing left later.

Stamina keeps you in

Three face-down stamina cards let you survive a move you can’t beat, and everyone can see who’s running low.

One small deck

One 60-card deck, 2–5 players, three 5-minute rounds.

Rules in brief

How it plays

The full rules, ready to read or print.

Download the rules (PDF)View

Setup

Deal each wrestler a hand and three face-down stamina cards. Two players use ranks 1 to 8 and 15-card hands, leaving 12 cards unseen; three use ranks 1 to 9 and 15 cards; four use the whole deck and 12; five use the whole deck and 9. Arrange your hand however you like.

Moves

A move is 1 to 4 cards: a set of one rank, or a run of 2 to 4 in a row. More cards beat fewer. With the same number of cards, a set beats a run; otherwise the higher number wins, with runs compared by their top card.

Your turn

If the table is empty, open with any move. Otherwise beat the move on the table, or spend stamina: a reversal takes a card off the move (an end card from a run) and discards a stamina card; a kick out takes a stamina card into your hand. No kick out with two players.

Takeback

If your move comes all the way back to you untouched, take one card from it back into your hand for free; the next wrestler answers what’s left. A move that was reversed is live, even for you.

Going down

Play your last card and you tap out. Have to spend stamina with none left and you’re pinned. Either way you’re out of the round, and the last wrestler standing wins it.

The match

Three rounds. The last one standing scores the number of players, the first out scores 1. The round winner opens the next. Most points wins; ties share the belt.
