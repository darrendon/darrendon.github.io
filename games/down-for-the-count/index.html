Down for the Count

A shedding game for 2–5 players where you don’t want to shed. Each turn, beat the move on the table or spend stamina to stay in. Run out of cards or stamina and you’re out. The last wrestler standing wins the round.

Overview

About the game

It’s a climbing game where an empty hand knocks you out. On your turn, beat the move on the table with a bigger one or spend one of your three stamina cards: a reversal takes a card off the move, and a kick out puts a stamina card into your hand. Play your last card and you tap out. Need stamina with none left and you’re pinned. The last wrestler standing wins the round.

An empty hand loses

In most climbing games you race to empty your hand. Here your last card knocks you out, so you have to choose when to play strong or weak melds to knock out your opponents and be the last one standing.

Three stamina cards each

Everyone gets three face-down stamina cards at every player count. Each reversal or kick out uses one and gets you a card. Stamina sits in front of you, so everyone can see who is close to being pinned.

Big moves cost cards

A move of three or four cards is hard to beat, but it uses up three or four cards. It pins a wrestler with no stamina left. Anyone else can spend stamina to stay in, and picks up a card doing it.

One deck for 2 to 5 players

Sixty cards, ranks 1 to 10, six of each. Two players remove the 9s and 10s and leave 12 cards unseen. Three players remove the 10s. Four or five use the whole deck.

Ten moves, six of each

Each rank is a wrestling move, from a Chop at 1 to a Frog Splash at 10, with the top-rope moves from 8 up. Four cartoon wrestlers appear across the cards: Big Moe, Rocket Ruiz, The Duchess and Hacksaw Hal.

Rules in brief

How it plays

The full rules, ready to read or print.

Download the rules (PDF)View

Setup

Deal each wrestler a hand and three face-down stamina cards. Two players use ranks 1 to 8 and 15-card hands, leaving 12 cards unseen; three use ranks 1 to 9 and 15 cards; four use the whole deck and 12; five use the whole deck and 9. Arrange your hand however you like.

Moves

A move is 1 to 4 cards: a set of one rank, or a run of 2 to 4 in a row. More cards beats fewer. With the same number of cards, a set beats a run; otherwise the higher number wins, with runs compared by their top card.

Your turn

If the table is empty, open with any move. Otherwise beat the move on the table, or spend stamina: a reversal takes a card off the move (an end card from a run) and discards a stamina card; a kick out takes a stamina card into your hand. No kick out with two players.

Takeback

If your move comes all the way back to you untouched, take one card from it back into your hand for free; the next wrestler answers what’s left. A move that was reversed is live, even for you.

Going down

Play your last card and you tap out. Have to spend stamina with none left and you’re pinned. Either way you’re out of the round, and the last wrestler standing wins it.

The match

Three rounds. The last one standing scores the number of players, the first out scores 1. The round winner opens the next. Most points wins; ties share the belt.
