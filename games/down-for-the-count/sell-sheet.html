Wrestling card game · 2–5 players

A shedding game where you don't want to shed!

Every card in your hand is a move you can still make, so you'd rather keep them. Each turn you top the move on the table or spend stamina to stay in the match. Play your last card and you tap out. Run out of stamina and the ref counts you out. The last wrestler standing wins the round.

Top the move
›

Play more cards than the move on the table, or the same number of higher ones. Two 7s beat two 5s.

Or spend stamina

Can't beat it, or would rather save your cards? Steal a card off the move, or pull a stamina card into your hand. You only get three.

1! 2! 3!

Out of stamina with no answer, you're pinned. Out of cards, you tap out. Three quick rounds, most points wins.
