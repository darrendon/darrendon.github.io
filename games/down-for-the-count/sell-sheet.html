Down for the CountShedding · Last One Standing

A shedding game where you don’t want to shed

It’s a climbing game where an empty hand knocks you out. Each turn, beat the move on the table or spend one of your three stamina cards to stay in. Run out of cards or stamina and you’re out. The last wrestler standing wins the round.

For fans of Scout and Love Letter

Beat the move
Two 5s
Two 7s beat it
Three 3s beat that

More cards beats fewer. With the same number of cards, the higher move wins.

Or spend stamina

Instead of beating the move, take a card off it or put a stamina card into your hand. You get three stamina cards a round.

Pinned

Need stamina with none left and the ref counts three. Last one standing scores the most; most points after three rounds wins.

2–5players
