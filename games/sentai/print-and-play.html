SENTAI

Print and play · 2–4 players · 20–30 minutes · cooperative · by Darren Don

Setup

  1. Sort Emperor Gorvax’s 18 monsters into Episode 1, Episode 2 and the Finale by their backs. Shuffle each episode and draw, face down: 3, 3 and 4 with 2 Heroes; 3, 4 and 5 with 3 Heroes; 4 each with 4 Heroes.
  2. Use one more color than you have Heroes. Shuffle those Hero cards and deal 15 each. Leftovers go back in the box, unseen.
  3. Lay the Episode 1 monsters face up in a line. Pick a first lead.
  4. From here on, no table talk: never say or hint at what you hold or want.

A fight

  1. The front monster names a combo and its size for 2, 3 and 4 Heroes (the dots).
  2. Starting with the lead, each Hero takes one turn: add 1 or 2 cards that fit, or pass.
  3. Beaten: the combo is complete. Discard the monster and the combo.
  4. Missed: the cards go back to whoever played them, every Hero discards 1 face up, and you fight it again.
  5. The lead passes left after every fight.

Combos

Run: different ranks that fit in a row (gaps are fine while you build), any colors. Set: one rank. Flush: one color. Roll Call: all different colors.

Winning and losing

Beat the last monster of the Finale and the squad wins. If any Hero’s hand is ever empty, the Boss wins. You may never add your last card.

Vael, the Hydra

Deal 14 each. Keep two heads out (three with 3 or 4 Heroes). On your turn add 1 or 2 cards to one head, or 1 card each to two heads; cards stay put. After each round: a finished head is cut and replaced; an unfinished head turns sideways (enraged); an unfinished head that was already enraged strikes: it and its cards are discarded and every Hero discards 2. Each head’s hex lasts until the head is gone.

Hero powers

Optional and easier: deal each Hero one power card, face up.

Printing. Print pages 2–25 double-sided at 100% (no scaling), flipping on the long edge, on card stock. Each odd sheet is nine card fronts and the next page is their backs. Cut along the grey lines. Or print the fronts only and sleeve the cards over opaque sleeves, keeping each episode of Boss cards in its own pile. The full rulebook is at darrendon.com/games/sentai.
