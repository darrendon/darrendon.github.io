Sentai

A cooperative card game where nobody talks about their hand and nobody ever draws. The squad builds each combo together, one or two cards at a time. Every card you spend is gone for good, and if any Hero runs out of cards, everyone loses. Fight as one.

About the game

Every Hero holds a hand of cards in the squad’s colors, ranked 1 to 12. Each season lines up its monsters face up, and each one wants a combo: a Run, a Set, a Flush or a Rainbow, sized for your squad. Starting with the lead, each Hero adds one or two cards that fit, or passes. Finish the combo and the monster is beaten. Fall short and the cards come back, everyone discards one, and you fight it again. Beat the Finale to win; if any Hero runs out of cards first, everyone loses.

No table talk

Nobody may say what they hold or what they want. The lead’s first card is the only signal, and every card that comes back after a miss shows the squad who holds it.

Your hand is your health

Nobody ever draws. Every card that beats a monster is spent, and if any Hero’s hand runs out, the Boss wins.

Two Bosses, optional powers

Emperor Gorvax sends a line of monsters, one fight at a time. Vael, the Hydra keeps two or three heads out at once, each with a hex. Five Hero powers make either one easier. 2–4 players in 20–30 minutes.

Five machines, sixty cards

Each Hero color is a machine: the Ember Lion, Tide Shark, Volt Falcon, Grove Rhino and Star Crane, each ranked 1 to 12. Play with one more color than you have Heroes. Boss cards print their combo and its size for 2, 3 and 4 Heroes.

Setup

Use one more color than there are Heroes and deal everyone 15 cards (14 against the Hydra). Each season shuffles its six Boss cards and draws a few, face down: 3, 3 and 4 with 2 Heroes; 3, 4 and 5 with 3; 4 each with 4. From here on, no table talk.

The fight

Lay the season’s monsters face up in a line and fight them in order. Each wants a Run (ranks that fit in a row, any colors, gaps allowed while building), a Set (one rank), a Flush (one color) or a Rainbow (all different colors). Starting with the lead, each Hero adds 1 or 2 cards that fit, or passes.

Beat it or fight again

Complete the combo and the monster is beaten; those cards are spent. Fall short and every card goes back to the Hero who played it, every Hero discards 1 face up, the lead passes left and you fight it again.

Winning and losing

Nobody ever draws. If any Hero runs out of cards, everyone loses. You may never add your last card to a combo, so a hand only runs out by discarding. Beat every monster in the Finale and the squad wins together.

Vael, the Hydra

Two heads are out at once (three with 3 or 4 Heroes), each with a hex. Add 1 or 2 cards to one head, or 1 card each to two heads; cards stay put. A finished head is cut off and a new one grows. A head left standing is enraged, and if it survives another round it strikes: its cards are lost and every Hero discards 2.

Hero powers

Optional, one per Hero. Command: when you lead, add up to 3. Riptide: once, nobody discards. Overcharge: once, one card fits any combo. Steady: you discard 1 fewer. Beacon: once a season, show a card.
