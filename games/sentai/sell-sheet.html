SENTAICooperative Shedding · 2–4 players

A cooperative card game where nobody talks and nobody ever draws

Why it stands out
  • The squad builds every combo together, one or two cards at a time. With no table talk, the cards you play are the only way to tell your teammates what you hold.
  • Your hand is your health. Every card that beats a monster is spent, a missed monster comes back until you beat it, and if any Hero runs out of cards, everyone loses.
  • Two Boss decks that play differently and five optional Hero powers, all in 101 cards: 2–4 players in 20–30 minutes.

For fans of The Crew and Woo

Face the monster

Each season’s monsters line up face up. Every card prints a combo (a Run, Set, Flush or Rainbow) and its size for 2, 3 and 4 Heroes.

Build it together

Starting with the lead, each Hero adds 1 or 2 cards that fit, or passes. Nobody may say what they hold: the first card played is the signal.

Beat it or fight again
→

Finish the combo and those cards are spent. Fall short and they come back, everyone discards 1, and you fight it again.
