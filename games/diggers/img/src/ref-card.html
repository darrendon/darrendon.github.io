Field Guide

On your turn, do one

Excavate

Play 1 to 4 adjacent cards from your hand to a site beside you: a run (3-4-5) or a set (all one number). To take a claimed site, beat its find: more cards, then suited run (one species) over set over run, then higher total.

Poach

Take an end card from a find beside you into your hand, then offer any card to the rival across that site. Kept, or out for the season.

Winning

Most prestige after three seasons. Tie: most seasons you ended by emptying your hand, then most prestige in season 3.

A season ends the moment any hand is empty.
