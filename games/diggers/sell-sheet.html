Card game · 2–4 players · 15–20 minutes

Your hand is locked. Their finds aren't.

Rival paleontologists share a ring of dig sites, one between each pair of camps. You win a site by laying a better find there: a run or a set of cards that sit side by side in your hand, because you can never rearrange it. Missing a bone? Take it from a rival. When anyone's hand runs out, the season ends and every site you hold scores.

A suited run beats that

More cards always wins. Same count? A suited run, all from one skeleton, beats a set, and a set beats a run.

Or poach
their side your side

Take an end bone off a rival's find and slide it anywhere into your hand. Then send them a card back. They keep it, or it's out for the season.
