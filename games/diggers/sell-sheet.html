On your turn, do one
  1. 1
    Excavate

    Play a run or set of cards that sit side by side in your hand. Beat the find on a site and it's yours.

  2. 2
    Or poach

    Take an end card off a rival's find. Send them one of yours.

  3. 3
    Go out

    Empty your hand to end the season. Sites score 1, then 2, then 4.

DIGGERSShedding lane battler · 2–4 players · 15–20 min

Outdig the camp next door. Poach the bones you need.

Race to empty your hand, but when anyone goes out, only the dig sites you hold score. Take a site with a better run or set, played from a hand you can never rearrange. For fans of Scout and Schotten Totten.

Lanes for up to four

A site between every pair of neighbors, so it plays as a duel or a four-way ring.

A race with teeth

Going out ends the season for everyone, locking in the sites you hold.

Nothing is safe

Any find you lay can be stripped for parts, and the rival hands you junk back.

2–4players
15–20minutes, three seasons
45cards, 4 site boards
