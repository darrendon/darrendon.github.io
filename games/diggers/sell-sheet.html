On your turn, do one
  1. 1
    Excavate

    Play cards that sit side by side in your hand as a run or a set. Beat the find at a site beside you and the site is yours.

  2. 2
    Or poach

    Take an end card off a rival's find, slot it anywhere in your hand, and send them one of yours.

  3. 3
    Go out

    Empty your hand and the season ends. Every site you hold scores 1, then 2, then 4 prestige.

DIGGERSShedding lane battler · 2–4 players · 15–20 min

Outdig the camp next door. Poach the bones you need.

Rival paleontologists race to empty their hands, but when anyone goes out, the season ends and only the dig sites you hold score. Every pair of neighboring camps shares a site, and you take it with a better run or set, played from a hand you can never rearrange. For fans of Scout and Schotten Totten.

Lanes for up to four

Most lane battlers are two-player only. Here a site sits between every pair of neighbors, so one deck plays as a duel or a four-way ring.

A race with teeth

Going out ends the season for everyone. Shedding isn't just a race to finish; it's how you lock in the sites while you're ahead.

The theme scores

Every card is a bone, and each species' nine cards make a full skeleton. More cards wins, and a run from one skeleton outranks a set.
