Diggers

A contested-lane climbing card game for 2–4 players. Rival paleontologists claim the dig sites between their camps with finds played from a hand they can never sort. Dig it up before they do.

Overview

The pitch

Between every pair of neighboring camps lies a dig site, and it belongs to whoever brought up the finer find there last. You play runs and matched sets from a hand you are never allowed to sort, take bones from the ends of your rivals' finds, and hand a specimen across in return. When anyone's hand runs out, the season closes and every site you hold scores. Three seasons, each worth more than the last.

A locked hand

Only cards sitting side by side can be played together, so every turn is a small puzzle. Poaching lets you slot exactly the bone you need into the gap.

Every site is shared

A find is safe until a neighbor brings a bigger one, and its end bones are theirs to take. The card you send back can be a gift or dead weight.

A built-in arc

Sites are worth 1, 2, then 4 prestige while finds grow from 3 to 5 cards. Season three can still turn the game: in simulated play it overturns the leader about a third of the time.

One small deck

Two players share three sites; three or four sit in a ring with a site between each pair. Same rules, same box.

Season 1Each site worth 1 prestige. Finds up to 3 cards.
Season 2Each site worth 2. Finds up to 4 cards.
Season 3Each site worth 4, as much as the first two together. Finds up to 5.

Components

Six dinosaurs, nine bones each

Every number is a bone. The six copies of each number come from six dinosaurs, each set in its own rock and marked with its own symbol. Species is only art; it never changes play.

The slabs

Lay any species' cards out 1 2 3 / 4 5 6 / 7 8 9 and the whole skeleton comes out of the rock. A spine played at a dig site shows a run of connected bones; a bone bed of 6s is three hips from three different animals.

Rules in brief

How it plays

The full rules are in the print-and-play PDF.

Setup

With two diggers, remove the 8s and 9s and lay three dig-site markers between you; deal 12 cards each. With three or four, use all 54 cards and lay one marker between each pair of neighbors; deal 11 or 10. Pick your cards up in the order dealt and never rearrange them.

Finds

A find is one or more cards sitting next to each other in your hand: a lone bone, a spine of consecutive numbers in order (3-4-5 or 5-4-3), or a bone bed of one number. More cards beat fewer; at equal size a bone bed beats a spine; then the higher total wins.

Excavate

Lay a find at a site beside your camp. An empty site takes any find; a claimed site, yours or a rival's, needs a find that beats the one there, which is discarded. The site is now yours.

Poach

Take one end card of a find at a site beside you and insert it anywhere in your hand. Then send any card from your hand to the digger across that site. They insert it anywhere, or turn it away and it's out for the season.

Seasons

The moment any hand is empty, the season ends and each site you hold scores: 1 prestige in season one, 2 in season two, 4 in season three. Finds may run up to 3, then 4, then 5 cards. Everything is cleared and fresh hands are dealt.

Winning

Most prestige after three seasons. Ties go to whoever ended the most seasons by emptying their hand, then to the most prestige in season three.
