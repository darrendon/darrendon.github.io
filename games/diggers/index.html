Overview

About the game

It’s 1877, during the Bone Wars. Each pair of neighboring camps shares a dig site, and the site belongs to whoever played the best find there last. You pick up your cards in the order they’re dealt and never rearrange them, so you can only play cards that already sit side by side. To fix your hand, you can take a card off a rival’s find. When anyone empties their hand, the season ends and every site you hold scores.

Beat the find
A run
A set beats it
A suited run beats that

More cards win. At equal size, a suited run (all one species) beats a set, and a set beats a run. After that, the higher total wins.

Or poach
their side your side

Take the end card off a rival’s find and put it anywhere in your hand. Then give them a card from your hand. They can add it to their hand or turn it away, which removes it for the season.

When someone plays their last card, the season ends immediately. Each site you hold scores 1 prestige in the first season, 2 in the second and 4 in the third.

Components

Five skeletons, nine bones each

Each card shows part of one of five skeletons, with its own rock color and symbol. Lay a species’ nine cards in a grid, 1 2 3 / 4 5 6 / 7 8 9, and they form the whole skeleton. Each game uses one more species than there are players.

The dig sites

Six real quarries from the Bone Wars

Each site is a half-size card laid between two camps, and your finds go on your side of it. They are named for quarries where Cope’s and Marsh’s crews competed.

Advanced: site effects. Every site card has a second side with a rule of its own. Once you know the game, shuffle the six each season and deal them effect side up, so the same hand plays differently at each site.

Rules in brief

How it plays

The full rules are two pages.

Download the rules (PDF)View

Setup

Use one more species than there are players. With two, use three species, lay three dig-site cards between you and deal 12 cards each. With three or four, use four or five species, lay one site card between each pair of neighbors and deal 11 or 10 cards each. Pick your cards up in the order dealt and never rearrange them.

Finds

A find is one or more cards sitting next to each other in your hand: a single card, a run of consecutive numbers in order (3-4-5 or 5-4-3), or a set of one number, up to 4 cards. More cards beat fewer. At equal size, a suited run (every card one species) beats a set, and a set beats a run. Then the higher total wins.

Excavate

Lay a find at a site beside your camp. An empty site takes any find; a claimed site, yours or a rival's, needs a find that beats the one there, which is discarded. The site is now yours.

Poach

Take one end card of a find at a site beside you and insert it anywhere in your hand. Then send any card from your hand to the digger across that site. They insert it anywhere, or turn it away and it's out for the season.

Seasons

As soon as any player’s hand is empty, the season ends and each site you hold scores: 1 prestige in season one, 2 in season two, 4 in season three. Everything is cleared and fresh hands are dealt.

Winning

Most prestige after three seasons. Ties go to whoever ended the most seasons by emptying their hand, then to the most prestige in season three.

Advanced: site effects

Each season, shuffle the six site cards and deal them effect side up. Hard Rock and Shallow Pit limit the size of finds, Trackway ranks runs above sets, Overturned Beds lets the lower total win, at Shared Claim a tie takes the site, and at Open Pit a poacher discards a card instead of sending one.
