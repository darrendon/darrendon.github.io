Overview

Dig it up before they do

It's 1877 and the bone rush is on. Every pair of neighboring camps shares a dig site, a lane the two of you fight over, and it belongs to whoever laid the best find there last. You pick up your cards in the order they're dealt and never rearrange them, so the only finds you can play are the ones already sitting side by side. When the bone you need is on a rival's find, you take it. When anyone's hand runs out, the season ends and every site you hold scores.

A suited run beats that

More cards always wins. Same count? A suited run, every bone from one skeleton, beats a set, and a set beats a run. Then the higher total.

Take an end bone off a rival's find and slide it anywhere into your hand, then send them a card back. They keep it, or it's out for the season. A good gift can be poison.

Components

Five skeletons, nine bones each

Every card is a bone from one of five animals, each set in its own rock with its own symbol. Lay a species' nine cards out 1 2 3 / 4 5 6 / 7 8 9 and the whole skeleton comes out of the slab. Each game uses one more species than there are players.

The dig sites

Four real quarries from the Bone Wars

Each site is a punchboard strip, the width of a card, laid between two camps. Your finds go on your side of it. The four boards are named for places where Cope's and Marsh's crews raced each other in the 1870s.

Rules in brief

How it plays

Setup

Each dig-site board is a lane between two camps. Use one more species than there are diggers. With two, play with three species and lay three dig-site boards between you; deal 12 cards each. With three, use four species; with four, all five. Lay one board between each pair of neighbors and deal 11 or 10. Pick your cards up in the order dealt and never rearrange them.

Finds

A find is one or more cards sitting next to each other in your hand: a single card, a run of consecutive numbers in order (3-4-5 or 5-4-3), or a set of one number, up to 4 cards. More cards beat fewer. At equal size, a suited run (every card one species) beats a set, and a set beats a run. Then the higher total wins.

Excavate

Lay a find at a site beside your camp. An empty site takes any find; a claimed site, yours or a rival's, needs a find that beats the one there, which is discarded. The site is now yours.

Poach

Take one end card of a find at a site beside you and insert it anywhere in your hand. Then send any card from your hand to the digger across that site. They insert it anywhere, or turn it away and it's out for the season.

Seasons

The moment any hand is empty, the season ends and each site you hold scores: 1 prestige in season one, 2 in season two, 4 in season three. Everything is cleared and fresh hands are dealt.

Winning

Most prestige after three seasons. Ties go to whoever ended the most seasons by emptying their hand, then to the most prestige in season three.
