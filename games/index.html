Every game here has a sell sheet, a rulebook and a version you can play right in your browser, with a tutorial and bots. Print-and-play kits and prototypes are available on request.

Dim Sum Rush

Each team shares face-up tiles the other team can’t touch, so every tile you put out is a play only your partner can make. A free-for-all mode plays at every count from 2 to 6.

For fans of Scout and Rummikub

  • 2–6 Players
  • 30–45 Min
  • Tiles or Cards
  • Partnership Climbing
  • Free-for-All Mode
See the game

Boo Who?

Your card faces the table, so everyone knows your role but you. Read the Boos piling up in front of you, then run or stay.

For fans of Scape Goat and Skull

  • 4–8 Players
  • 15 Min
  • Bluffing
  • Party
See the game

Too Many Crooks!

Crew up from the dice your rivals send over, then pay them from what you kept back. Promises are out loud, cuts are secret, and nothing is binding.

For fans of Intrigue and Batam

  • 3–6 Players
  • 20–30 Min
  • Dice
  • Negotiation
See the game

Diggers

Fight over the dig sites between camps with runs and sets from a hand you can never rearrange, and poach the bones you need.

For fans of Scout and Schotten Totten

  • 2–4 Players
  • 15–20 Min
  • Shedding
  • Lane Battler
See the game

Down for the Count

A shedding game where you don’t want to shed. Beat the move or spend stamina to stay in; the last wrestler standing wins.

For fans of Scout and Love Letter

  • 2–5 Players
  • 15 Min
  • Shedding
  • Last One Standing
See the game

Un, Deux, Trois, Cat!

Everyone flips at once. Grab the felt toy shown in its own color before anyone else, or grab the cat when nothing is.

For fans of Ghost Blitz and Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza

  • 2–6 Players
  • 10 Min
  • Cards and Felt Toys
  • Dexterity
