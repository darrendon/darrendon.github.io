Games.

Each game has a sell sheet, a rulebook and a playable version. Print-and-play kits and prototypes are available on request.

All games

Diggers

A lane battler where you can never rearrange your hand. Climb on a rival’s dig site to take it, or poach one of their cards to strengthen your hand at a cost.

2–4 players · 10–20 min · Shedding Lane Battler · Tuck box, $10–15

For fans of Scout and Schotten Totten

See the game

Dim Sum Rush

A partnership climbing game where you can only help your partner through the face-up tiles you share. Works with tiles or cards, plus a free-for-all mode.

2–6 players · 30–45 min · Partnership Climbing · Two-piece box, $20–30

For fans of Tichu and Rummikub

See the game

Too Many Crooks!

A dice negotiation game where your score depends on the dice other players decide to send you. You pay your crew with the dice you kept, and no deal is binding.

3–6 players · 20–30 min · Dice Negotiation · Two-piece box, $20–30

For fans of Intrigue and Batam

See the game

Down for the Count

A shedding game where you don’t want to shed. Beat the move on the table or spend stamina to stay in. The last wrestler standing wins.

2–5 players · 15 min · Shedding · Two-piece box, $10–15

For fans of Scout and Love Letter

See the game

Un, Deux, Trois, Cat!

A dexterity game where you grab cat toys. Everyone flips a card at once; grab the toy shown in its own color, or the cat if none is. In the optional Cat Paw edition, you bat them with cat paws.

2–6 players · 10 min · Dexterity · Two-piece box, $20–30

For fans of Ghost Blitz and Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza

See the game

Sentai

A co-op card game where your hand is your health. Play cards together to beat a line of monsters, but you can’t talk about your hand and you never draw. If anyone runs out of cards, everyone loses.

2–4 players · 20–30 min · Cooperative Hand Management · Two-piece box, $20–25

For fans of The Crew and Sky Team

See the game
