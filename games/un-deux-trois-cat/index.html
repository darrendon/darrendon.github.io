Un, Deux, Trois, Cat!

A dexterity game for 2–6 players with five felt cat toys and a felt cat. Everyone flips a card at once. If a card shows a toy in its own color, grab that toy first. If none does, grab the cat.

Overview

The pitch

Everyone chants “un, deux, trois, cat!” and flips their top card together. Each card shows one of five toys, usually in another toy’s color. If any card shows a toy in its own color, grab that toy from the middle of the table. If none does, grab the cat.

Felt toys to grab

Five felt cat toys and a felt cat sit in the middle of the table, and players grab the toys themselves. The card art is painted to match them.

The answer can be on any card

Players check every card on the table, including their own. More players means more cards and more rounds with two right answers. A fast player can grab both.

Decoy colors

Each off-color card uses another toy’s color, so a green mouse draws your eye to the fish and a blue bird to the ball. Players have to check the toy and its color before they grab.

Two to six players

Deal out all 60 cards and play until they’re gone, 10 to 15 rounds. With two or three players, everyone flips two cards each round.

One toy in its own colorGrab that toy. The green fish is a fish in its own color.
Two toys in their own colorsBoth are right, and each one is a point. Grab both if you’re fast enough.
Nothing in its own colorGrab the cat. It’s never on a card.

Components

Five toys, five colors

Each toy has its own color: a yellow bird, a green fish, a blue ball, a pink mouse and an orange butterfly. Each one appears on twelve cards, three in its own color and nine in the other toys’ colors.

The mouse in all five colors. Only the pink one is a mouse in its own color.

Rules in brief

How it plays

The full rules, ready to read or print.

Download the rules (PDF)View

Setup

Put the five toys and the cat in the middle, in everyone’s reach. Deal out all 60 cards face down: 30 each for two players, 20 for three, 15 for four, 12 for five, 10 for six. Hold your cards face down in your hand.

The flip

Everyone chants “un, deux, trois, cat!” and on “cat!” flips the top card from their hand face up in front of them. With two or three players, everyone flips two cards.

What to grab

If any card shows a toy in its own color, grab that toy. If several toys do, each is a correct grab, and you can grab more than one if you’re fast enough. If none does, grab the cat.

Scoring

Each correct toy you grab is a point. Score it by putting a flipped card face down in your point pile: your own first, then any other flipped card nobody has claimed. The rest go on the discard pile.

Wrong grabs

If you grab the wrong thing, let go. You sit out the rest of the round and lose a point: one card from your point pile goes on the discard pile.

Game end

Everyone runs out of cards on the same round. Most points wins. If players tie, deal everyone one more card from the discards and flip once more. Only the tied players may grab.
