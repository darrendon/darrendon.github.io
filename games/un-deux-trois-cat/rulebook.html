A dexterity game · 2 to 6 players · about 10 minutes

Un, Deux, Trois, Cat!

Everyone flips a card at once. Spot a toy shown in its own color and grab it before anyone else does. Nothing in its own color? Grab the cat.

Object of the game: grab the right toy first, round after round. When the cards run out, the player with the most points wins.

In the box

60Cards, poker-sized: twelve per toy. Each toy appears on 3 cards in its own color and 9 in other toys’ colors. 6Cat toys: a bird, fish, ball, mouse and butterfly, each in its own color, and a cat. 1Rulebook

Every toy has its own color. The cat has none: it’s the grab when no toy shows in its own color, and it never appears on a card.

Setup

  1. Put the five toys and the cat in the middle of the table, where everyone can reach them.
  2. Shuffle all 60 cards and deal them out face down, as shown below.
  3. Hold your cards face down in your hand. Your point pile goes in front of you.

Playing a round

  1. Everyone chants together: “Un… deux… trois… cat!”
  2. On “cat!” everyone flips the top card from their hand face up in front of them. Flip it away from you, so you don’t see it before anyone else.
  1. Look at every face-up card on the table and grab the right thing from the middle (see the next page).
  2. Score the round, put the toys back in the middle and chant again.

With 2 or 3 players, everyone flips two cards each round. All of them count.

What to grab

A toy shown in its own color, on any card: grab that toy. It doesn’t matter whose card it is.

Several different toys shown in their own colors: every one of them is a correct grab, and you can grab more than one if you’re fast enough. Once a toy is in someone’s hand it’s taken.

No toy shown in its own color: grab the cat.

Scoring a round

Each correct toy you grab is one point. For each point, put a flipped card face down in your point pile: your own flipped card first, then any other flipped card nobody has claimed. The rest go on a shared discard pile.

If two hands land on the same toy, the hand underneath got there first.

Wrong grabs

Grab the wrong thing and you let go straight away. You’re out for the rest of the round, and you lose a point: put one card from your point pile on the discard pile. No points yet? Nothing happens.

Ending the game

Everyone’s hand runs out on the same round. The player with the most points (cards in their point pile) wins.

Tied? Shuffle the discard pile and deal one card face down to every player. Chant and flip once more. Everyone’s card counts, but only the tied players may grab. The first correct grab wins. Still nobody? Do it again.

Example round

Four players flip a pink bird, a green fish, a blue butterfly and a yellow mouse. Only the fish is in its own color.

  • Ben grabs the fish first. That’s one point: he puts his own flipped card face down in his point pile.
  • Cleo grabs the cat. That’s wrong, because a toy was in its own color. She lets go and puts one card from her point pile on the discard pile.
  • The other three flipped cards go on the discard pile. Put the toys back and chant again.

Good to know

  • Two correct toys? One fast player can take both, for two points.
  • No peeking at your card before “cat!”.
  • The cat is never on a card. It’s only ever the grab when no toy is in its own color.

Quick reference

Chant and flip together on “cat!”. Check every card.

A toy in its own color: grab it. Several? Each is a point.

None: grab the cat.

Wrong grab: out for the round, lose a point.

Most points when the cards run out wins.

Cat Paw edition

In the Cat Paw edition, each player has a cat paw: a plush paw on a wooden stick. Nobody touches the toys with their hands.

  • Hold your cat paw as you flip.
  • On “cat!”, use your paw to bat or drag the right toy to yourself. Once you have it, it counts as your grab.
  • Bat the wrong toy to yourself and it’s a wrong grab.

Everything else is the same.
