A dexterity game · 2 to 6 players · about 10 minutes

Un, Deux, Trois, Cat!

Everyone flips a card at once. Spot a toy shown in its own color and grab it before anyone else does. Nothing in its own color? Grab the cat.

What you need

60 cards from this kit, and five toys and a cat. The finished game comes with felt cat toys. For this kit, cut out the toy tokens on the last page, or use any five small things in the five colors and anything at all for the cat.

Each toy appears on 12 cards: 3 in its own color and 9 in other toys’ colors.

Setup

Put the five toys and the cat in the middle of the table, where everyone can reach them. Shuffle all 60 cards and deal them out face down. Hold yours face down in your hand. Points go in a separate pile in front of you.

A round

  1. Everyone chants together: “Un… deux… trois… cat!”
  2. On “cat!” everyone flips the top card from their hand face up in front of them. Flip it away from you, so you don’t see it before anyone else.
  3. Look at every face-up card on the table and grab the right thing from the middle (see the next page).
  4. Score the round, put the toys back in the middle and chant again.

With 2 or 3 players, everyone flips two cards each round. All of them count.

What to grab

A toy shown in its own color, on any card: grab that toy. It doesn’t matter whose card it is.

Several different toys shown in their own colors: every one of them is a correct grab, and you can grab more than one if you’re fast enough. Once a toy is in someone’s hand it’s taken.

No toy shown in its own color: grab the cat.

Scoring a round

Each correct toy you grab is one point. For each point, put a flipped card face down in your point pile: your own flipped card first, then any other flipped card nobody has claimed. The rest go on a shared discard pile.

If two hands land on the same toy, the hand underneath got there first.

Wrong grabs

Grab the wrong thing and you let go straight away. You’re out for the rest of the round, and you lose a point: put one card from your point pile on the discard pile. No points yet? Nothing happens.

Ending the game

Everyone’s hand runs out on the same round. The player with the most points (cards in their point pile) wins.

Tied? Shuffle the discard pile and deal one card face down to every player. Chant and flip once more. Everyone’s card counts, but only the tied players may grab. The first correct grab wins. Still nobody? Do it again.

Good to know

  • Two correct toys? One fast player can take both, for two points.
  • No peeking at your card before “cat!”.
  • The cat is never on a card. It’s only ever the grab when no toy is in its own color.
