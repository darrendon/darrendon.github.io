Un, Deux, Trois, Cat!

Everyone flips at once. Find a toy shown in its own color and grab it before the cats beside you do.

Opponents
Bots
  1. Any card shows a toy in its own color: grab that toy. If several different toys show their own color, each one can be grabbed, and everyone who grabs one scores.
  2. No card shows a toy in its own color: grab the cat.
  3. Grab a correct toy and keep your flipped card. Grab the wrong thing and you're out for the round, and you discard one of your scored cards.
  4. When the decks run out, most cards wins. Ties go to a sudden death flip.

Play online

Friends join from their own phones or computers with a table code. Your browser runs the game, and bots fill any empty seats.
