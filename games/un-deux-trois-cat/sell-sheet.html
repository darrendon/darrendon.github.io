Un, Deux, Trois, Cat!Dexterity · 2–6 Players · 10 Min

Everyone flips a card at once. Grab the toy in its own color, or the cat.

Players chant “un, deux, trois, cat!” and flip their top cards together. Each card shows one of five toys, usually in another toy’s color. If any card shows a toy in its own color, grab that toy from the middle of the table. If none does, grab the cat. The answer depends on every card on the table, so it changes every round.

For fans of Ghost Blitz and Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza

Un, deux, trois…

Everyone flips. Pink bird, orange ball, blue butterfly, yellow mouse: no toy is in its own color.

…Cat!

Everyone grabs for the felt cat. The first player to get it scores a point.

Next round

The green fish is in its own color, so the fish is the grab. When two toys are in their own colors, each is a point, and a fast player can grab both.
