About プロフィール

Hi, I’m Darren.

I’m a board game designer in Kansas City, Missouri, with games signed with Allplay, Bitewing Games and SRG Universe. Here’s what I hold every design to:

  1. 1
    Every move is about someone else.

    An offer only your partner can take, a die you send to a rival, a Boo you put on a friend. If you can play your turn without looking up, it isn’t done yet.

  2. 2
    Teach it in five minutes.

    Short rules and small boxes, with the depth showing up on the second play, so it actually gets to the table.

  3. 3
    Start from games people love.

    I take the parts of my favorite games that work best and put them together in a way nobody has played before.

Every game plays in your browser with a tutorial and bots, so your team can try it before a prototype ships.

Built for online tables

Dim Sum Rush and Too Many Crooks! already host online tables your team can join with a five-letter code. The goal is for every game here to work the same way, so you can play a full game together from anywhere.
