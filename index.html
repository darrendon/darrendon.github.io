Hi, I’m Darren.

I’m a board game designer in Kansas City, Missouri, with games signed with Allplay, Bitewing Games and SRG Universe. Here’s my design philosophy:

  1. 1
    Start from games people love.

    I start from mechanics players already know and create something new.

  2. 2
    Keep the rules simple.

    I aim for rules you can teach in a few minutes with as few components as possible without compromising depth of gameplay.

  3. 3
    Design for the whole table.

    I aim for turns where you have to pay attention to everyone else at the table, because the best moments happen in the interaction between players.
