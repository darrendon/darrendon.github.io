About

Hi, I’m Darren.

I’m a board game designer in Kansas City, Missouri. I’ve been designing for over a year, and I have games signed with Allplay, Bitewing Games and SRG Universe, with titles to be announced.

To me, board games are a way for people to connect with each other. My favorite games are the ones that make you look across the table and ask yourself, “What are they thinking?”

When I design, I start from the games I love and ask how to take the best of them and put them together in a way nobody has seen before. Diggers, for example, plays a Scout-style hand you can never rearrange across Schotten Totten-style lanes.
