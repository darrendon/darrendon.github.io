About

Hi, I'm Darren.

I'm the designer behind What's Good Board Games. Most of my work is in card games, especially shedding and climbing games, with party, dexterity and negotiation games alongside them.

I like games that are quick to teach and full of decisions: what to play, what to hold, and what to give your partner. I build every design as a playable digital version early, so I can test it in volume and share it with anyone, then take it back to the table to see how it feels with real people.

If you're looking for a game for your line, or want to playtest something new, I'd love to hear from you.
