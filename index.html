About

Hi, I’m Darren.

I’m a board game designer in Kansas City, Missouri, with games signed with Allplay, Bitewing Games and SRG Universe. Here’s my design philosophy:

  1. 1
    Start from games people love.

    I take the parts of my favorite games that work best and put them together in a way nobody has played before.

  2. 2
    Keep the rules simple.

    Rules you can teach in a few minutes and components that fit in a small box, with the depth showing up on the second play, so the game actually gets to the table.

  3. 3
    Every move is about someone else.

    The best moments happen between players: a deal, a bluff, a gift that turns out to be a trap. If you can take your turn without looking up, the design isn’t done yet.
